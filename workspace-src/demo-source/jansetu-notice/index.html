Public Notice 04/2026 12 May 2026

JanSetu Education Support Pilot

The Nirmal Pradesh Student Welfare Board invites applications for the JanSetu Education Support Pilot for the 2026 academic year.

1. Benefit

Eligible applicants may receive a one-time education support grant of ₹8,000. This is not a recurring stipend.

2. Eligibility

  • The applicant must be enrolled in Class 12 at a recognised school.
  • The applicant must be ordinarily resident in Nirmal Pradesh.
  • Annual household income must not exceed ₹2,50,000.

3. Application window

Applications open on 15 May 2026 and close at 17:00 on 30 June 2026. Late applications will not be accepted.

4. Scope

This is a limited pilot of the fictional Nirmal Pradesh Student Welfare Board. It is not a nationwide Government of India programme.

— digitally issued — Secretary, Student Welfare Board
